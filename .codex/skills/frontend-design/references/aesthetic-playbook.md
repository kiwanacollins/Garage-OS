# GarageOS Aesthetic Playbook

Pick one direction per surface. GarageOS should feel capable, current, and operational, not like a generic SaaS template.

## Service Bay Command

Best for admin dashboards, front desk queues, work-order lists, and dispatch screens.

- Mood: controlled service floor, clean tools, crisp lighting, steady pace.
- Palette: `#F8FAFC` background, `#FFFFFF` surfaces, `#3857A3` structure, `#EE1E24` urgent accents, slate neutrals.
- Layout: left nav, dense top toolbar, split workspace, sticky action rail, tables/list rows with strong scan columns.
- Typography: Inter, 600-700 headings, mono for IDs/plates/time.
- Motion: row selection reveal, inspector slide-in, quiet save/status transitions.

## Mechanic Job Card

Best for mechanic workflows, inspection, labour logging, parts requests, and completion.

- Mood: practical, glove-friendly, focused, a little more tactile.
- Palette: light neutral base, primary blue headers, warning amber for awaiting parts/attention, green for completed work.
- Layout: job card list, detail tabs, sticky status/action bar, large inputs for findings, photo grid, timer module.
- Typography: clear labels, compact headings, mono values for work order IDs and timers.
- Motion: timer pulse, upload progress, tab transition, completion confirmation.

## Customer Service Portal

Best for customer appointment booking, service tracking, invoices, history, and feedback.

- Mood: reassuring, transparent, polished, less dense than staff tools.
- Palette: white and soft primary tints, brand blue for trust, red only for payment due or urgent notice.
- Layout: status timeline, vehicle selector, invoice list, appointment slots, concise explainer copy.
- Typography: slightly more spacious line height and section spacing than staff screens.
- Motion: step progress, status timeline reveal, appointment slot selection.

## Owner Analytics

Best for revenue, staff performance, reports, exports, and business health.

- Mood: executive clarity without glossy decoration.
- Palette: primary blue navigation, neutral chart surfaces, semantic colors for state; avoid rainbow dashboards.
- Layout: KPI strip only when it supports decisions, chart + table pairing, date filters, export queue/status.
- Typography: strong numeric hierarchy, restrained headings, mono for deltas and ranges.
- Motion: filter transition, chart redraw, export progress.

## Public Brand Moment

Best for marketing, public home, login/register polish, and explainers.

- Mood: modern auto-service confidence, real workshop context, trustworthy.
- Palette: brand blue dominant, red as action or highlight, dark overlay only when imagery needs contrast.
- Layout: full-bleed automotive/service imagery, narrow text column on a calm image area, one CTA.
- Typography: brand/product name is loudest; support copy stays short.
- Motion: hero entrance, subtle image depth, CTA/focus transition.

## Avoid

- Generic dashboard mosaics made only of cards.
- Decorative gradients behind routine operator screens.
- Multiple accent colors competing with status semantics.
- Stock-like imagery that does not show vehicles, service, people, or garage context.
- Copy that sounds like a homepage inside a staff workflow.
